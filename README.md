# Melbl8 Clock06 Office Triptych Weather

Three-panel multilingual Aurecon office clock for the fixed **3840 × 804** Melbourne gallery display.

## Live site

https://creative-innovation-labs-bmc.github.io/Melbl8-Clock06-Office-Triptych-Weather/

## Behaviour

- Melbourne remains in the centre and changes its wording every minute.
- Side offices rotate every 30 seconds.
- The right office is offset by 15 seconds so the two sides never change together.
- Each panel has three typed PT Serif lines.
- The third line gives a short Melbourne weather/time-of-day observation or compares the office time with Melbourne.
- Current temperature and a short weather condition appear at the right of each English title row.
- Weather is requested from Open-Meteo and cached locally. A weather failure never hides the clock.

## Typography

- Serif message text: locally hosted **PT Serif Bold**.
- Sans-serif titles and utility text: locally hosted **Open Sans**.
- Font binaries and SIL Open Font Licence files are stored in `assets/fonts`.

## Display and preview

- Production canvas: `3840 × 804`.
- Mobile landscape previews scale the complete fixed canvas.
- Portrait phones display a rotate prompt.

## Privacy

The page includes `noindex`, `nofollow`, `noarchive`, `nosnippet` and a site-wide `robots.txt` disallow rule.
